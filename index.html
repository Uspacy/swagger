Uspacy  is a next-gen agentic AI workspace for communication, collaboration, and sales. All-in-one.

Uspacy is also a modern platform that gives developers incredible flexibility!

Our product is built on an API-based architecture. From the very beginning, we created an open and well-documented API that we use ourselves. This means any solutions developers want to build to extend Uspacy’s capabilities will be based on the same principles as the core product.

With the Uspacy API, the platform can be integrated with thousands of products worldwide, making the work of small and medium-sized businesses more flexible and efficient.
